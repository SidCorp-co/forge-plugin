## The stages — the rung an issue is judged at, and what no rung buys

Every rung runs the same statuses in the same order. Claim a rung by meaning, and the claim is the
author's: a `trivial` is one tree with no hook or skill file, no screen and no migration; a `fix` is
one behaviour and its replacement, carrying no rule; a `feature` is everything else. Ask
`forge advance <ref> --owed` what the rung in hand costs.

No rung buys away a judgement; the two below `feature` buy away its repetition. The review, a
verdict on every criterion, and the gate the ship spends cost a `trivial` what they cost a
`feature`, because the shortest change breaks a tree as well as the longest. What those two stop
paying is the second of a kind already spent — and which ones, for the issue in hand, is
`forge advance <ref> --owed`, off the one table that grants them. `trivial` and `fix` are granted
the same set and differ by the ceiling the ship measures the landing against.
