## Reconstructing the state

Four steps, in this order, and they are the shape of every turn this method takes.

**Read the world.** Not the journal of what was attempted — the world: what the deployment reports,
what the branch points at, what answers and with what. The record says what was intended and what has
been tried; only the world says where things now stand, and the two disagree exactly when something
has gone wrong, which is the case you are here for.

**Compare it with the intended state.** The job's prompt says where the release was going. Hold the
reading beside it and say, in one sentence a person could check, what is different.

**Name the gap.** One gap, stated as a difference and not as a cause. *Production answers, and it
answers as the build from before this release* is a gap. *The deploy failed* is already a diagnosis
and usually a wrong one.

**Choose the smallest action that closes it.** Smallest by blast radius, not by effort: the action
that would leave the least changed if it turns out to be the wrong one. A larger action that would
also have closed the gap is the action that makes the next reading uninterpretable, because two
things moved and nothing says which one mattered.

Then read the world again. A run that acts twice before it reads has lost the thread of what its own
first action did, and no amount of care in the second action gets it back.
