## Phase 4 — File, and report

**Nothing that proposes work is posted before a second model has read it.** A filing naming a cause
or an implementation detail carries the consult it survived; the one thing exempt is a line that
gives a figure's direction and names neither a cause nor work to do, which leaves nobody anything to
argue with. The consult is `forge codex consult`, taken on the draft and never on the filing already
posted: a reading on the issue is read by the next run as evidenced, whatever came back afterwards.

**What goes up is the reasoning and not the conclusion.** The two window values, the counts on both
sides, what the attribution rests on — the change named, and what rules out the other differences
between the windows — and the proposed work in the words it would be filed in. Beside them goes the
reading's own statement of what none of its angles measures, quoted from under the angle
block of `forge stats eval` rather than paraphrased here, because a board handed the figures and
not that sentence weighs a proposal against a set whose limit nobody told it. A conclusion sent alone
is reviewed on its plausibility, which is the failure this step exists to prevent.

**More than one angle, and the figure decides which rather than the model that read it.** `tech` and
`ba` on every one of them: the first for whether the named change could have moved that figure at
all and what else between the windows could, the second for whether the two values and the counts
bear the claim as it is worded. `user` on top of those where the proposal changes text a run meets —
a guide sentence, a refusal's wording, a default, a gate's scope — the run reading it being the one
who pays. `ux` where what moves is something a person reads on a screen. The filing names the angles
it was read at.

**What comes back goes into the filing, the disagreement included.** A finding taken changes the
draft before it is posted. A finding turned down is posted with it, together with the reason it was
turned down: an attribution that survived an objection is worth more than one nothing argued with,
and the rejection is the half a later reader has no other way to get.

**Where the consult cannot be had** — no credential, a rate limit, a reply that never came — the
filing says which of those happened and stands as unreviewed. Nothing is read in its place: a model
of the reading model's own family, or a second pass by the evaluator over its own draft, buys the
appearance of this step and none of what it is for.

A figure read as *worse* becomes a filing on the issue whose change it is attributed to, through
`forge comment`, carrying the two values, the counts and the attribution. A
figure read as *better* is reported and not filed. Nothing goes to the knowledge store: what was
learned about a change belongs on that change's issue.

A saving Phase 3b found becomes a filing on the issue that owns the text or the check it names, or a
new enhancement where none does, carrying the count of runs that paid and what each paid.

Report one line per figure — what moved, which way, the reading, the angles it was read at and the
key filed if any — one line per saving proposed with its count and its key, and one line naming what
the windows were and what qualified them.
