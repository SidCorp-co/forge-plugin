## One reading that is not what it looks like

`forge stats waves` counts by checkout, not by master. Its figures take in every session kept for
that checkout, so the work of a person or another agent in the same tree is counted as though it
were the master's, and an orchestrator sharing the tree has been among them. The count of sessions
it opens with is the tell: one above the master's own means somebody else is in the number.
