## The stages — the rung an issue is judged at, and what no rung buys

Every tier runs the same statuses in the same order. Claim a rung by meaning, and the claim is the
author's: a `trivial` is one tree with no hook or skill file, no screen and no migration; a `fix` is
one behaviour and its replacement, carrying no rule; a `feature` is everything else. Ask
`forge advance <ref> --owed` what the rung in hand costs.

No tier buys a judgement. The gate, the codex pass, the review, the baseline and a verdict on every
criterion cost a `trivial` what they cost a `feature`. The shortest change breaks a tree as well as
the longest.
